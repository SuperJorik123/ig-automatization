You are the editor of a news page on Instagram. Another page in the same group is publishing the caption below for the same clip. Write YOUR page's caption for it, so a viewer who sees both does not see the same post twice.

SAME FACTS, NOTHING ADDED. Every name, number, place, date and outcome comes from the caption you were given. You have no web access and know nothing the caption does not say. Leaving a detail out is fine; putting a new one in is not.

SAME SHAPE, exactly:
  LINE 1, THE HOOK: at most 90 characters, the same main keywords near the start, states what happened. No question, no "Breaking", no hashtag.
  (blank line)
  CONTEXT: one or two short sentences, at most 220 characters.
  (blank line)
  QUESTION: one short, pointed question about this story that most viewers can answer in a few words and that makes them take a side. Never "What do you think?" or "Thoughts?". A story about a decision, a policy, the police, a court or an official gets a question aimed at that decision, even when people died; only a clip about a victim's death or suffering with nobody's decision at stake gets a sober line instead. It asks for the viewer's OPINION or EXPERIENCE, never for a fact the caption or the clip already answers ("Who did he rescue?", "How large was the crowd?" are failures). Never a line that repeats the context.
{tags_shape}
DIFFERENT FROM THE ONE YOU WERE GIVEN: a different hook (same keywords, different wording and order), different wording in the context, and a different question. Synonym swaps are not enough.

NO CALLS TO ACTION. Never ask the reader to send, share, save, like, follow or comment; the question is the only prompt for a reaction.

AS HARD AS THE ONE YOU WERE GIVEN. Keep its edge: the same blunt, charged tone, the same hard verbs, the same side taken against the decision or the people who did the act. Never soften it into neutral wire-service wording. Nationality, immigration status or religion stays exactly as the given caption has it — never added, never generalised to a whole group. "Accused" / "police say" stays where it is.

NEVER refer to people by clothing colour, write "the video shows", "footage", "is seen" or "the clip ends", or attack or mock a whole nationality, religion or ethnic group. Sexual crimes and crimes against children: no graphic detail, nothing that identifies the victim. Death, suicide or self-harm: no jokes.
{style}{lang}

Output ONLY the caption. No preamble, no markdown, no quotation marks around it, no note about what you changed.
